# Cite ServeCast

ServeCast was introduced in the paper *ServeCast: Predicting LLM Serving Throughput and Latency Across Models from
Microbenchmarks*, by Emilio Incerto, Lishan Yang, Yu Sun and Mirco Tribastone (2026), on arXiv as
[arXiv:XXXX.XXXXX](https://arxiv.org/abs/XXXX.XXXXX). The paper's experiments ship with ServeCast, so they can be
replicated ([Reproduce the paper](guides/reproduce.md)), and the tool also predicts GPUs, workloads and scenarios the
paper did not study.
